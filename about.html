Our story

About Us

Ponderosa Pine Trading 26CC is a proud third-generation civil engineering company based in Shelly Beach, KZN, with expertise spanning decades across South Africa and beyond.

Our journey

From Johannesburg to the South Coast

Established originally in Johannesburg under the name Dick Smith. Since 1976, after relocating our offices to Phalaborwa, we have served regions including Venda, Gazankulu, Lebowa, Kruger National Park, Mozambique, and surrounding areas.

Our long-standing presence demonstrates commitment, expertise, and reliability in every project we undertake.

  1. The beginning

    Johannesburg

    Founded as Dick Smith, working across the Cape, Free State, Natal and Transvaal.

  2. 1976

    Phalaborwa

    Offices relocated, serving Venda, Gazankulu, Lebowa, the Kruger National Park and Mozambique.

  3. Today

    Shelly Beach, KZN

    Delivering major projects for NPC, Idwala Carbonates, Rossmin Mine and Overport Agri.

Our expertise

What we build

We specialize in civil engineering construction including retaining walls, crusher bases, columns, beams, suspended slabs, thickeners, reservoirs, concrete roads, weighbridges, silo bases, as well as residential and commercial buildings. Each project is executed with precision, safety, and quality as our guiding principles.

Our experience

Who we build for

Over the past 18 years, we have successfully completed projects locally at NPC, Idwala Carbonates, and Rossmin Mine, as well as in the Western Cape for Overport Agri. Our portfolio showcases versatility and reliability across both industrial and commercial sectors.
