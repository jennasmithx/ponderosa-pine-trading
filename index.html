Our Expertise

We specialize in comprehensive civil engineering construction, from retaining walls and crusher bases to thickeners, reservoirs, and commercial or residential builds. Our reputation is built on precision, reliability, and craftsmanship.

Over the past 18 years, we have proudly completed major civil projects for NPC, Idwala Carbonates, and Rossmin Mine locally, as well as projects in the Western Cape for Overport Agri.
