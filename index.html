Who we are

Precision, reliability & craftsmanship

Originally established in Johannesburg under the name Dick Smith, we’re a proud third-generation company now based in Shelly Beach, KZN. Since relocating to Phalaborwa in 1976, we’ve served regions including Venda, Gazankulu, Lebowa, the Kruger National Park, and Mozambique.

Over the past 18 years, we have proudly completed major civil projects for NPC, Idwala Carbonates, and Rossmin Mine locally, as well as projects in the Western Cape for Overport Agri.
